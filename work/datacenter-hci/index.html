Datacenter: 3-Tier vs HCI

My final-year project at CBI.MA: two ways to build a datacenter, compared on a working mock-up with data protection on top.

Where CBI.MAWhen 2025 – 2026School EMSI, Rabat

Context

CBI.MA is an integrator specialised in datacenter, cloud and cybersecurity infrastructure: 55 years in business, 300+ people, 13 African countries.

The question: should a customer build on the classic 3-Tier architecture (separate compute, network and storage) or on hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI), where the same nodes provide both? I built both and measured them.

Architecture

Simplified view of the two mock-ups and the shared backup layer.

What I built

  • A 3-Tier mock-up: VMware ESXi 8.0 hypervisor, with SAN/NAS storage on TrueNAS Core (ZFS pool in RAID-Z1, iSCSI and NFS LUNs).
  • A hyperconverged architecture on Nutanix Community Edition, managed through a Prism Element cluster.
  • Data protection with Veeam Backup & Replication: full and incremental backups, restores, and native Nutanix AOS snapshots.
  • Benchmarks of I/O performance and of backup and restore times, run on both architectures.
  • A 5-year TCO analysis, and architecture recommendations presented to the CBI.MA teams.
