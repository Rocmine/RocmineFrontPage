What runs here

  • Multi-node virtualisation with Proxmox VE: web services, monitoring tools and game servers (Minecraft, CS2, FiveM).
  • Local LLMs with Ollama, and experiments with AI agents connected to tools through MCP.
  • An Nginx reverse proxy with load balancing, behind Cloudflare protection.
  • Full Linux administration: network configuration, access management, backups, and hardening of exposed services.
  • It hosted the Morocco Earthquake Tracker through more than 10 million visits, without interruption.
